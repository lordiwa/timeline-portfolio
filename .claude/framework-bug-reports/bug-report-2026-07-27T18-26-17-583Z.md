# Reviewer is told to audit a pre-hand-off checklist the protocol never delivers to it

## What happened
The hivemind:reviewer briefing instructs it to audit the developer's 5-item pre-hand-off checklist (unspecced path exercised, red-green plant, dist rebuild, parity copies, calibration markers). The reviewer runs in a fresh context and never receives that checklist, because nothing in the protocol passes it. Three separate reviewers in this session raised it as a finding and had to condition their verdict on it. One classified it as a procedural MEDIUM and asked the orchestrator to confirm the items out of band. The friction is that the developer DOES produce the checklist correctly, with a per-item outcome, in its own final report to the orchestrator. The artifact exists; there is simply no channel from developer to reviewer. The orchestrator would have to manually copy it into the review prompt, and nothing in the protocol documents that as a required step. The net effect is a recurring spurious finding on every ticket, consuming reviewer attention and orchestrator turnaround, and diluting the signal of the reviewer's real findings.

## What was expected
Either (a) the protocol should define how the developer's pre-hand-off checklist reaches the reviewer (e.g. persisted on the ticket, written to the session bundle, or an explicit documented orchestrator step of relaying it), or (b) the reviewer briefing should not ask it to audit an artifact it has no way to obtain, and should treat the checklist as the orchestrator's responsibility rather than a reviewable item.

## Steps to reproduce
1. Dispatch a hivemind:developer against a ticket. Observe that its final report contains the 5-item pre-hand-off checklist with an outcome per item.
2. Dispatch a hivemind:reviewer in a fresh context against the resulting commit, following the standard protocol (do not manually paste the developer's checklist into the review prompt).
3. Read the reviewer's report.
4. Observe it reports the checklist as not provided and conditions a finding on it.
5. Repeat on the next ticket. The same spurious finding recurs.

## Environment
hivemind 0.18.0; Windows 11 Pro 10.0.26200; PowerShell 5.1; project is Vue 3 + Vite 5 + Phaser 3 with Vitest; observed across 4 review dispatches on 2 tickets in a single orchestration session; reviewers dispatched with model fable per project convention
